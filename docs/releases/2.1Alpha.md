**中文**：2.1Alpha 在首次引导中新增“选择手环管理软件”步骤。用户在允许 Android 通知权限后，可选择小米、华为、荣耀、OPPO 或 vivo；选定品牌时，应用会自动尝试启动该品牌已安装的手环管理软件。返回本应用后，用户可继续完成引导并开始小说通知发送。

**English**: 2.1Alpha adds a “Select wearable companion app” step to onboarding. After Android notification permission is granted, users can select Xiaomi, HUAWEI, HONOR, OPPO, or vivo. Selecting a brand automatically attempts to launch its installed wearable companion app. After returning to this app, users can complete onboarding and begin novel notification delivery.

## 主要变更 / Key changes

- 首次引导由 3 步扩展为 4 步，并在通知权限之后加入品牌选择步骤。 / Expanded onboarding from three to four steps, adding brand selection after notification permission.
- 支持小米、华为、荣耀、OPPO 与 vivo 的手环／手表管理软件启动。 / Supports launching companion apps for Xiaomi, HUAWEI, HONOR, OPPO, and vivo wearables.
- Android 原生层按候选包标识顺序查找已安装应用，并通过 `PackageManager.getLaunchIntentForPackage()` 启动。 / The Android native layer looks up installed apps through candidate package IDs and launches them via `PackageManager.getLaunchIntentForPackage()`.
- 若未检测到对应管理软件，会自动尝试打开应用商店；无法打开时会显示可读错误提示。 / If no compatible companion app is found, the app attempts to open the application store and otherwise shows a readable error.
- Android 清单新增手环管理软件包可见性声明，以适配 Android 11 及以上的包可见性限制。 / Added package-visibility declarations for wearable companion apps to support Android 11+ package-visibility restrictions.
- 设置页“关于”区域更新为 `2.1Alpha（2.1.0-alpha.2+3）`。 / Updated the Settings About section to `2.1Alpha (2.1.0-alpha.2+3)`.
- 品牌选择现在会自动应用保守分段预设：小米 160 字、华为 80 字、荣耀 80 字、OPPO 100 字、vivo 100 字、其他品牌 60 字。 / Brand selection now applies conservative segment presets: Xiaomi 160, HUAWEI 80, HONOR 80, OPPO 100, vivo 100, and Other 60 characters.
- 新增“其他品牌”选项，不会尝试启动未知管理软件，而是应用 60 字通用预设并提示用户手动完成通知同步。 / Added an Other-brand option that applies the 60-character fallback without attempting to launch an unknown companion app.
- 选择品牌时会立即保存分段字数；完成引导并返回书库后，当前图书会以新字数重新分段，旧自定义分段与发送断点会被清除，避免混用不同预设。 / Choosing a brand immediately persists the segment length. After onboarding returns to the library, the current book is re-segmented and legacy custom chunks and sending progress are cleared to prevent mixed presets.

> 选择品牌会设置可在统一设置中继续调整的保守字数建议。手环的实际显示容量随型号、固件、手机通知栏预览和配套软件而变化；请仍在管理软件的“设备 / 通知 / 应用通知（或应用提醒）”中为“手环通知小说”开启通知同步或镜像。 Brand selection applies an adjustable conservative recommendation. Actual display capacity varies by model, firmware, phone notification preview, and companion app; notification sync or mirroring for **手环通知小说** must still be enabled under the companion app’s device-notification settings.
